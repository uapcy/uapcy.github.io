The UAP Public Database (UPDB) is an open-access resource that we are building to share validated observations, case files, and analytical findings with researchers, journalists, and the public.

Coming Soon

The UPDB is currently under development. Once launched, it will provide searchable access to case reports, observational data, and analytical documents. Check our Announcements page for updates.

Data Principles

Our database is guided by three principles: transparency (data is shared openly), privacy (witness identities are protected unless they consent otherwise), and rigour (only verified and peer-reviewed observations are published).
