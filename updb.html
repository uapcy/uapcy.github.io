Open Database

Public resources for UAP case data and government documents

The following external databases provide public access to large collections of UAP case data, government documents, and related research materials. Each resource is maintained by an independent organisation and offers a different approach to cataloguing the phenomenon — from AI-driven case aggregation to searchable archives of official government releases.

External Public Databases

PHENOMaiNON — Cases
AI-driven case database · 252,001 case files
A cloud-native AI platform from PHENOM LLC that aggregates UAP case reports into a single browsable master record. Users can filter cases by country, state, object shape, theme, date range, and minimum number of corroborating sources. Each case entry links through to its full case file and underlying references.
Visit PHENOMaiNON Cases →
The Black Vault — UFO Files Search Engine
Official US government release archive · 446 records
A searchable archive of official UFO/UAP document releases from the United States government. Each document has been processed with OCR so its full text is searchable. The collection contains documents, videos, and images, and can be filtered by agency, release batch, and file metadata.
Search the archive →
UAP Records Archive
Independent machine-readable catalog
A machine-readable dataset cataloguing US government UAP records and linking directly to their authoritative sources — including the National Archives, AARO, ODNI, NASA, FBI, and CIA. It includes a case layer of official case designations and a media inventory of assets referenced across government publications.
Visit the archive →
