Our data collection combines instrumented observations, volunteer reports, and validated witness testimonies. Every piece of data is logged, timestamped, geolocated, and stored for analysis.

Instrumented Observation

We operate optical camera stations distributed across Cyprus, providing continuous monitoring of the sky. Additional sensor data is collected from partner networks and citizen scientists.

Public Reports

Anyone can submit a UAP sighting report via our Report a UAP form. Reports are reviewed by our investigative team, cross-referenced with instrument data where possible, and added to the database (with the witness's permission).

Verification Process

All reported cases pass through a multi-step verification process: initial intake, cross-referencing with aviation and meteorological data, instrument correlation, and peer review. Findings are published on our site and shared with the wider research community.
