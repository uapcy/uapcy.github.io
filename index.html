UAP Cyprus is an all-volunteer, non-profit research centre dedicated to the scientific investigation of Unidentified Anomalous (also known as Aerial) Phenomena (UAP) occurring in the airspace and maritime domains of the Republic of Cyprus, and in the Eastern Mediterranean region. The work of our interdisciplinary team is dedicated to the pursuit of truth in a scientific manner and is subject to peer-reviewed validation.
