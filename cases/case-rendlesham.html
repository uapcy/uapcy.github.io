Rendlesham Forest

1980 · Suffolk, United Kingdom

← Back to all Important UFO Cases
Date
26–28 December 1980
Location
Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, UK
Type
CE2 — Physical traces & radar

Description

Over three consecutive nights in late December 1980, United States Air Force personnel stationed at RAF Bentwaters and RAF Woodbridge in Suffolk, England, reported observing unusual lights and a landed craft of unknown origin in the nearby Rendlesham Forest.

The most widely documented incident occurred in the early hours of 26 December, when two security patrolmen — Staff Sergeant Jim Penniston and Airman First Class John Burroughs — were sent to investigate lights seen descending into the forest. According to their testimony, they encountered a small, triangular metallic craft bearing symbols on its side. Penniston reported touching the craft and later transcribed a sequence of binary digits he claims appeared on its surface.

On the night of 28 December, Deputy Base Commander Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt led a second investigation into the forest. He carried a portable tape recorder and documented the event in real time; the recording captures him and his team observing pulsing lights, reporting radiation readings above background levels, and witnessing objects maneuvering through the trees. Halt later filed an official memorandum to the UK Ministry of Defence describing the events as unexplained.

The Rendlesham Forest incident remains one of the most thoroughly documented and officially acknowledged UAP cases in history. Physical traces — including indentations in the forest floor and elevated radiation readings — were recorded at the landing site. The case has been the subject of multiple government inquiries, books, and documentaries, and it remains formally unexplained.
