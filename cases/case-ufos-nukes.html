UFOs and Nuclear Facilities

1940s–present · Global

← Back to all Important UFO Cases
Period
1940s – present
Location
Global — nuclear facilities
Type
Multiple — pattern of reports

Description

Over the past seven decades, a recurring pattern of UAP sightings has been reported at or near nuclear weapons facilities, nuclear power plants, and missile silo complexes — most notably in the United States and the former Soviet Union. These reports have come from military personnel, civilian contractors, and in some cases from official government documents later released under freedom of information legislation.

The most thoroughly documented cases include reports of UAP hovering over nuclear missile silos at Malmstrom Air Force Base in Montana (1967), reports over RAF Bentwaters and RAF Lakenheath in the UK (1956, 1980), and numerous accounts from former military personnel in the US Strategic Air Command. In several documented cases, witnesses reported that their weapons systems were temporarily disabled during the sightings.

In September 2010, a press conference was held at the National Press Club in Washington DC, organised by researcher Robert Hastings, at which a group of former US Air Force officers and enlisted personnel publicly described their experiences with UAP at nuclear weapons facilities. Hastings has since catalogued over 160 such witnesses.

The pattern remains unexplained. Some analysts suggest it reflects surveillance interest in humanity's nuclear capabilities; others consider it a mix of genuine anomalous events and misidentification. The case is included here as an important theme in UAP research rather than as a single incident.
