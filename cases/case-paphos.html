The Flying Disc over Paphos

1954 · Paphos, Cyprus

← Back to all Cyprus Cases
Date
4 July 1954
Location
Paphos, Cyprus
Type
CE2 — Physical evidence

Description

On 4 July 1954, the Cypriot newspaper Κυπριακή reported that an English doctor observed a large metallic sphere emerging from the sea near Paphos. According to the report, the object surfaced from the water and then rose into the air, accelerating at tremendous speed.

Several other witnesses in the area independently confirmed seeing the object. This sighting is considered one of the earliest documented UAP reports in Cyprus involving a water-to-air transition — a phenomenon that has since been reported in numerous coastal sightings worldwide.

The object was described as "the size of a large disk," metallic, and completely silent during its ascent. Witnesses reported that the water around the emergence point was disturbed but returned to normal shortly afterward.

Source Material

Primary source: Κυπριακή newspaper, 4 July 1954.
