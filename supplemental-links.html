Supplemental Links

External platforms, research centres and channels on UAP and related phenomena

About This Page

The resources below are external platforms — research centres, foundations, archives, podcasts, and YouTube channels — that publish UAP and related content. They are maintained independently of the UAP Cyprus Centre and represent a range of perspectives and methodologies.

View with a critical eye. We list them as a starting point for further reading and research, not as endorsements of any particular claim or viewpoint.

Research Organisations & Foundations

The Sol Foundation
Science, policy, and public education for a post-UAP world
The Sol Foundation brings together experts from academia and government to address the philosophical, policy, and scientific problems raised by the likely presence on Earth of UAP. Its research agenda cuts across the natural sciences, the social sciences and humanities, engineering, and policy. Sol publishes white papers on transparency, national and global security, energy solutions, and space commerce and regulation.
Visit →
New Paradigm Institute
Disclosure advocacy through litigation and policy
The mission of the New Paradigm Institute is to inform the public of the true facts surrounding the UFO/ET phenomenon and to mobilize them to secure full and responsible disclosure of information about UFO technology and nonhuman intelligent visitors held in secret from Congress for 80 years by the military/intelligence/aerospace industry complex. The means employed will be strategic litigation, policy development and implementation, deep research, public and academic education, and national, international, and grassroots organizing.
Visit →
Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies (SCU)
Scientific research coalition
A coalition of scientists, researchers, and professionals from organizations, governments, and industries, working to conduct, promote, and encourage rigorous scientific examination of Unidentified Aerospace Phenomena (UAP), commonly known as Unidentified Flying Objects (UFOs). The coalition publishes peer-reviewed papers and maintains a public research library.
Visit →
The J. Allen Hynek Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS)
Non-profit scientific research organisation
CUFOS is a non-profit organization that includes scientists, professionals, academics, investigators, and others, all volunteers, dedicated to the study and analysis of the UFO phenomenon. Its purpose is to promote serious scientific interest, investigation, and research on UFOs, to serve as an archive for reports, documents, and publications about the UFO phenomenon, and to be an educational resource for the public.
Visit →
National UFO Historical Records Center
Historical archive and collections
The National UFO Historical Records Center brings together many valuable collections of records documenting the history of the UFO/UAP phenomenon. The historical collections include case files from: the National Investigation Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP), founded by Thomas Townsend Brown and Donald Keyhoe; the Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS), founded by J. Allen Hynek, including his personal Project Blue Book files; and the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization (APRO), founded by Jim and Coral Lorenzen.
Visit →
Lund University — Anomalous Agenda
Advanced Study Group · Pufendorf Institute
An advanced study group at Lund University's Pufendorf Institute for Advanced Studies, led by Prof. Anders Warell and PhD student Melina Tsapos. The group focuses on perception, stigma, and methodology in UAP research, contributing an academic perspective to the scientific study of the phenomenon.
Visit →
Danish Network for UAP Studies
Academic research network · Scandinavia
An academic network supporting UAP research initiatives across Denmark and Scandinavia, connecting researchers, institutions, and independent scholars working on the scientific study of UAP.
Visit →

SkyWatch — Passive Radar UFO Detector
Citizen-deployed radar network · developed by AAI
SkyWatch is a decentralized network of citizen-deployed radar receivers to generate a real-time, nationwide map of UFO flight patterns. AAI's Mitch Randall developed and deployed a proof-of-concept system with Harvard's Galileo Project as a Research Team member.
